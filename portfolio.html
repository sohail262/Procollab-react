2024
Inventory Management System
Farmer couldn't track stock across 2 shops. Built real-time inventory platform.
70% manual tracking time eliminated

The Problem

A family-owned retail business was losing 4 hours per day to manual spreadsheet tracking across 2 locations. Stock mismatches were common. Workers had no real-time visibility.

The Solution

Built a real-time inventory platform using React + Firebase that synced stock levels across locations instantly.

Before

4 hours/day manual work

Weekly stock mismatches

No delivery visibility

After

30 minutes/day of app usage

Zero mismatches

Real-time tracking

Key Learnings

  • Real-time databases need offline-first thinking
  • UX matters more than architecture
  • Users don't care about your tech stack

Hospital Resource Optimization
ERs struggled with bed allocation. Built AI-driven triage and bed management system.
1st Place AI4Health Competition

The Problem

Hospital emergency rooms couldn't allocate beds efficiently. Patient triage was slow. Emergency response times were unpredictable.

The Solution

AI-driven system that intelligently prioritizes patients and predicts bed availability. Real-time dashboard for administrators.

Impact

Won 1st place at AI4Health Competition. System demonstrated 40% improvement in emergency response times.

2025
ProCollab — Published Research
Engineering and pharmacy students worked in silos. Built AI platform to connect disciplines.
Published at IICTDS 2025 — EAI

The Problem

Students from different academic disciplines rarely collaborate. Engineering student needed designer. Pharmacy student needed developer. They never found each other.

The Solution

Built an AI-powered platform using DeepSeek R1 that intelligently matches students across disciplines based on skills, interests, and project requirements.
